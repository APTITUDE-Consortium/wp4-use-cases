## Standards and Protocols

This section consolidates the technical standards and protocols cited by the 11 WP4 use cases. Every entry below appears in at least one per-UC. The standards cited by the 11 use cases fall into five categories.

* **EUDIW framework, core protocols, and credential formats**
* **Trust framework, certificates, and credential status** 
* **Engagement and transport**
* **Sector-specific standards**
* **Cross-cutting legal references** 

### EUDIW framework, core protocols, and credential formats

* **EUDI Wallet Architecture and Reference Framework (ARF)**. Architectural baseline for wallet, issuer, relying party, and intermediary roles.
* **eIDAS 2.0 / European Digital Identity Wallet framework**. Trust model and legal baseline for issuers, wallets, verifiers, and intermediaries.
* **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2977** (PID attribute set). Mandatory PID attributes consumed at booking and check-in. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Verifiable Credentials (VC) data model**. Standard data model for representing identity, travel, and entitlement credentials. Cited by **UC 1, UC 3, UC 4, UC 5, UC 9, UC 11**.
* **OpenID for Verifiable Credential Issuance (OpenID4VCI) 1.0**. Issuance protocol from attestation providers to the wallet. Cited by all Use Cases.
* **OpenID for Verifiable Presentations (OpenID4VP) 1.0**. Presentation protocol from wallet to relying party, in remote and proximity bindings.
* **OpenID4VC High Assurance Interoperability Profile (OpenID4VC-HAIP), Draft 6/1** (OpenID Foundation). Interoperability profile applied on top of OpenID4VCI/OpenID4VP. Cited by **UC 6, UC 7, UC 8**.
* **OAuth 2.0**. Underlying authorization framework used by the issuance and presentation flows. Cited by **UC 1, UC 3, UC 4, UC 5, UC 9**.
* **OAuth 2.0 Attestation-Based Client Authentication (OAUTH-ATTESTATION), IETF Draft 7**. Wallet-attestation-based client authentication. Cited by **UC 6, UC 7, UC 8**.
* **OAuth 2.0 Multiple Response Type Encoding Practices (OAUTH-MULT-RESP, 2014)**. Multiple-response-type handling. Cited by **UC 6, UC 7, UC 8**.
* **ISO/IEC 18013-5** (mobile driving licence, mDL/mdoc). Used for proximity verification flows and as a credential format. Cited by Cited by all Use Cases.
* **ISO/IEC 23220 (PhotoID)**. Standardised data models and building blocks for mobile PhotoID credentials, including facial-image–based identity representations. Cited by **UC 1**.
* **SD-JWT VC**. Selective-disclosure credential format. Cited by **UC 4, UC 5, UC 6, UC 7, UC 8, UC 10, UC 11**.
* **JWT key binding (`cnf` claim) per SD-JWT VC**. Cryptographic binding of each EAA to the wallet instance. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Digital Credentials API (DC API)**. Browser- and OS-level interface for native invocation of the wallet for online credential presentation. Cited by **UC 1** (online and pre-departure flows) and **UC 3** (hotel flow, where supported).
* **EUDIW Intermediary Service pattern**. Architectural component that manages issuance and verification flows, performs trust/validity/freshness/revocation checks, and exposes APIs to sectoral back-end systems. Cited by **UC 3, UC 4, UC 5, UC 9**.
* **API-based integration with sectoral back-end systems**. API integration with airline check-in/boarding/flight-status/gate-control, ferry ticketing and boarding, hotel booking portals/DMC/PMS/self-service terminals, and university/service-provider systems. Cited by **UC 3, UC 9**.
* **Electronic Attestations of Attributes (EAA / QEAA)**. Cited by **UC 5** for representation of eligibility attributes such as student status.

### Trust framework, certificates, and credential status

* **RFC 5280** (Internet X.509 Public Key Infrastructure Certificate and CRL Profile). Cited by **UC 6, UC 7, UC 8**.
* **Trusted Issuer Lists per ARF / eIDAS 2.0 Implementing Acts**. Resolution of PID issuer trust (cross-border) and EAA issuer trust. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Qualified electronic seal under eIDAS 2.0**. Issuer signing of EAAs, with signature algorithms per ARF crypto suites. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **eIDAS Level of Assurance High**. Identity proofing assurance level for PID-binding events. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **IETF Token Status List, Draft 13**. Status-list-based revocation/validity mechanism for verifiable credentials. Cited by **UC 6, UC 7, UC 8**.
* **Status List / revocation mechanisms (eIDAS 2.0 aligned)**. Mechanisms to verify whether PID, DTC, and access credentials are still valid at presentation time. Cited by **UC 4, UC 5**.
* **DIACC Pan-Canadian Trust Framework (PCTF)**. Trust-framework reference used for Canadian-side assurance mapping. Cited by **UC 11**.

### Engagement and transport

* **ISO/IEC 18004 (QR Code)**. QR-based OpenID4VP engagement at kiosks and booking platforms. Cited by **UC 10**. QR-code-based engagement more generally is cited as a wallet engagement method by **UC 3, UC 9**.
* **ISO/IEC 14443 (NFC)**. NFC engagement between wallet and proximity readers. Cited by **UC 10** (door reader). NFC-based proximity interaction more generally is cited by **UC 3, UC 9**.
* **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**. Wallet-to-reader proximity engagement, complementary to NFC. Cited by **UC 10** (Phase 4 Room Key presentation).
* **TLS 1.3 (RFC 8446)**. Transport security for online wallet ↔ issuer and wallet ↔ relying-party backend channels. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Deep links / redirects / HTTPS-based wallet engagement**. Web- and app-based engagement patterns for moving the user from the service environment to the wallet for consent and presentation. Cited by **UC 3, UC 9**.

### Sector-specific standards

* **ICAO Doc 9303** (Machine Readable Travel Documents). Reference for name normalisation/transliteration handling between PID and hotel/authority systems. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Regulation (EU) 2025/12** (advance passenger / guest data). Applied to authority reporting via Guest Summary EAA. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **National hotel-registration laws (DE, FR, ES, IT transpositions)**. Authority reporting at the hotel touchpoint. Cited by **UC 10**.
* **Biometric QR / checkpoint-readable token pattern**. Pilot interaction model in which a protected biometric QR credential serves as a checkpoint-readable journey token. Cited by **UC 3** (airport episode) and **UC 9**.
* **1:1 biometric verification orchestration**. Live facial capture compared against a reference associated with the presented credential, rather than against a central biometric database. Cited by **UC 3** (airport episode) and **UC 9**.
* **TS12-aligned wallet payment authorization**. Wallet captures the user's payment authorization and binds it to transaction details. Cited by **UC 3**.
* **Strong User/Customer Authentication (SUA/SCA)**. Strong wallet-based user/customer authentication for the payment step. Cited by **UC 3**.
* **Dynamic Linking**. Cryptographic binding of the user's payment authorization to the concrete transaction context (amount, payee). Cited by **UC 3**.
* **Propagation to card-based or account-to-account rails via PSP/payment interfaces**. Forwarding of wallet-authorised payment evidence through the relevant payment path. Cited by **UC 3**.
* **EN 301 549** (Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services). Cited by **UC 11** on the EU side, with Quebec/Canadian equivalents on the Canadian side.
