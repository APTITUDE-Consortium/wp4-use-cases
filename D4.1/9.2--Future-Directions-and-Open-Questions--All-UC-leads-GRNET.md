### 9.2 Future Directions and Open Questions (UC 10)

**Out of scope for D4.1 / current pilot phase:**

- Integration with hotel loyalty programmes (cross-chain identity reuse)
- Payment integration via WP6 (booking payment is handled outside the wallet in pilot)
- Long-term credential lifecycle (multi-year guest history, repeat-stay credentials)
- Wallet-to-wallet (W2W) credential transfer (e.g., parent issues a sub-credential to a child's wallet)
- Hotel-to-hotel credential reuse (e.g., Booking EAA from one hotel gives discount at another)
- Integration with airport / transport (cross-scenario journey — handled by SEDIT-X UC 3)
- Production-scale TIL governance (pilot uses WP2 test TIL)

**Remaining technical questions:**

- Should Room Key EAA support delegation (guest delegates entry to a friend)?
- How does revocation propagate to door locks if IDnow's signing key is compromised mid-stay?
- How is offline TIL freshness reconciled with key rotation?

**Remaining legal questions:**

- Liability allocation when an EAA is honoured offline by a door lock with stale TIL data
- Legal status of Phase 5 minimised disclosure under each Member State's hotel registration law
- GDPR retention of consent logs by IDnow as QTSP
