### 9.1 Identified Risks and Mitigation Measures (UC 10 contributions)

| Risk ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R10 | Interoperability gaps between operators (e.g., Spanish wallet ↔ German hotel) | Medium | High | WP2 testbed conformance testing; SD-JWT VC + OID4VP standardisation; pre-pilot interop matrix |
| R4 | Scheduling overlap with other LSPs (POTENTIAL, WE BUILD) | Medium | Medium | Coordination calls with LSP leads; reuse of common credential schemas |

| UC10-R2 | National hotel registration law requires attributes not in PID | Medium | Medium | Legal review per pilot country; fallback to manual data entry for missing attributes |
| UC10-R3 | EUDIW market penetration too low for meaningful pilot | High | Medium | Recruit test travellers via consortium channels; provide pre-loaded reference wallets |
| UC10-R4 | QTSP qualified seal certificate not available in time | Low | High | IDnow legal to confirm certificate provisioning timeline; fallback to non-qualified seal for pilot if needed |
| UC10-R5 | Cross-border name normalisation fails (transliteration) | Medium | Medium | Fuzzy matching tolerance in hotel system; manual reconciliation fallback (UP2) |
| UC10-R6 | User refuses Phase 5 consent at scale → regulatory non-compliance for hotel | Low | High | Manual guest registration fallback; clear legal-basis communication in consent prompt |
